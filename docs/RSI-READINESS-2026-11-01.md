# Scientific self-improvement: evidence and present limits

Assessment date: 2026-11-01. **EIDOS has not demonstrated autonomous scientific
self-improvement, and no current model has earned scientific adjudication
authority.** This is a present acceptance decision, not a claim that future
systems cannot achieve it. Existing self-check, bounded service recovery,
registered experiment execution and manual controls remain available.

## What the evidence supports

- ScienceRSI-8b.i1-Q4_K_M scored 13/15 on structured S4 design judgement and
  17/20 on the held-out S7 support task. Substantive failures include confounding,
  statistical misuse and selection bias. These are historical 2026-10-27 short
  probes, not new measurements or a long-context research qualification.
- StartLux's recorded bounded routing calibration does not qualify experiment
  design, scientific novelty or final acceptance. The other legacy stage-model
  candidates have no accepted EIDOS scientific-task qualification.
- Softmax reconstruction and repair provide checkable evidence for the bounded
  numerical/experimental paths actually exercised. A known-paper reconstruction
  does not demonstrate discovering an unknown scientific gap.
- The independent HPAF metric-repair case supports bounded transfer of a
  check-and-repair workflow. It does not establish general autonomous discovery.
- Software tests and controlled recovery probes qualify their listed contracts.
  Their count is not a measure of scientific reasoning or self-improvement.

## Why extended development did not establish maturity

The recurring failure is confusing a declaration or local success with a full
capability: registered models with qualified reasoning, a callable agent with a
completed research lifecycle, passing code with correct scientific semantics,
and source qualification with a migrated live deployment. Wrong-run tracking,
measurement/verification clock confusion and publication-receipt gaps are
examples of contracts that the original tests did not exercise adequately.

LLMs used to implement and review the system remain fallible. Multiple roles
using similar models do not provide independent ground truth. A model may write
a test suite that shares the assumptions of its implementation. Review approval
must therefore be supplemented by independent references, deliberately broken
controls, held-out tasks and real user-path execution.

Development also expanded across environment tooling, agents, scientific cases,
delivery and presentation before the central research mission was qualified.
Repeated long regression runs cannot substitute for deciding which capability
is missing. The recent submission check's dependency on a shared external
temporary directory illustrates a verification-scope problem; unrelated files
must not be repeatedly moved merely to obtain a green commit.

## Operating decision

1. Keep the system supervised. LLMs may propose hypotheses, plans, code and
   objections. Registered tools execute; independent checks and explicit human
   scientific gates decide acceptance. Preserve monitoring and bounded recovery.
2. Complete and release the bounded current lifecycle with its limitations.
   Avoid additional feature expansion until the critical research-task evaluation
   is ready. Source, installed package and live-process scopes remain distinct.
3. Freeze a model/task evaluation protocol before tuning or comparison. Include
   long-source retrieval, faithful citations, falsifiable hypotheses, experimental
   design, executable correction, numerical traps and unsupported novelty.
   Hold-out cases and answers must be independent of the system under evaluation;
   deterministic references and domain reviewers supply ground truth.
4. Report invalid-claim acceptance, missed defects, valid-case rejection,
   abstention/coverage, source fidelity, executable results and uncertainty.
   Predeclare task-specific thresholds and adequate sample sizes. A small set
   with no observed failures is not a zero-risk guarantee. Retain every adverse
   result and record model/quantization, prompt, context, seed, source and data
   versions. Stronger candidates receive the same tests and no automatic promotion.
5. Count an improvement only when a locked candidate improves independently
   verified held-out research tasks and preserves accepted capabilities. Roll
   back regressions. Scientific novelty and critical design still require human
   review until separately justified.

The next milestone is a complete supervised research lifecycle plus an
independently assessed task-qualification result. “More agents,” more code,
higher self-reported confidence or a larger model alone does not meet it.
