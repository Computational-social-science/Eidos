## O1. The claim reproduces a number, but does it test the paper's method?

[RESOLVED] — The claim is deliberately narrower than the paper's: it asserts that the tool returns the
printed value on the printed input, and the negative control (an unnormalised teacher) shows the
zero-sum check can fail. The paper's training claims are out of scope and are listed as such in the
register rather than implied.
