# Referee simulation — surrogate reference study

## O1. The study may be read as a claim about real systems
`[RESOLVED]` **scope**

It is not one. `du/dt = λ − u²` is a test bed whose fixed point is known in closed form;
the register's `non_goals` say so, and the report repeats it. The result is about the
operator's ability to recover a known fixed point, which is exactly what a planted-truth
control is for.

## O2. A gate that cannot fail is not a gate
`[RESOLVED]` **control**

The same procedure is run with shuffled training labels. It recovers 1 of 19 branches
within 5% (5.3%) against 19 of 19 in the forward arm, so the audit can fail and does fail
when the information is destroyed. Both arms are stored in `docs/evidence.json`; neither
number is typed by hand.
